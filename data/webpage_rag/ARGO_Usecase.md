# Frequently Asked Questions

Source: https://federicab03.github.io/ARGO_Usecase/pages/faq.html

Working with Wayfarer

Everything you need to know about planning a retreat with us.

Have questions about customization, logistics, pricing, or capabilities? We've compiled answers to common inquiries.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

Questions answered. Doubts resolved. Every detail thoughtfully explained so you can plan with confidence.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend 3–6 months for corporate events to ensure venue and activity availability. Shorter timelines may be possible depending on dates and scale.

Can you accommodate groups of any size?

Our venues range from 20–150 guests. For larger groups or bespoke requirements, we can coordinate multiple venues or partner spaces.

Are meals included in pricing?

Yes. All Wayfarer packages include full catering with customizable menus. Dietary preferences and restrictions are accommodated at no additional cost.

What happens if weather is poor?

Both venues include fully equipped indoor spaces and weather backup plans. All outdoor activities have indoor alternatives that maintain program integrity.

Do you provide accommodation?

Yes. Both venues include overnight accommodation. Room options range from standard doubles to suites. We also arrange transportation to/from airports or rail stations.

What activities are available?

Options include guided hikes, storytelling sessions, workshops, live performances, cooking classes, and custom team-building experiences. We tailor the roster to your group's objectives.

Can we bring our own speakers or facilitators?

Absolutely. We host client-led sessions, guest speakers, and custom programming alongside our own activity roster. All technical and logistical support is provided.

What is your cancellation policy?

Deposits are non-refundable but can be applied to future dates. For full terms, we discuss cancellation conditions during your proposal consultation.

Do you handle transportation?

We arrange ground transportation between local airports and venues, plus any inter-venue or day-trip logistics your program requires.

# The White Tower Upper Gallery

Source: https://federicab03.github.io/ARGO_Usecase/venues/minastirith.html

Grand Alpine Venue

A remarkable hilltop setting combining historic architecture, panoramic city views, and elevated terraces for memorable gatherings, formal events, and distinctive presentations.

**The White Tower Upper Gallery**

A fortress of hospitality above the city. Grand, dramatic, and framed by sweeping views, the White Tower offers an unforgettable setting for occasions that call for distinction.

Perched high above the city with commanding panoramic views, The White Tower Upper Gallery offers a truly distinctive backdrop for an Alpine evening. Its elevated terraces and eastern balcony provide natural sightlines across the city walls, making the setting particularly compelling for acoustic performances and outdoor moments. The venue's historic architecture creates an atmosphere of grandeur, while its multi-level layout brings a unique character to the flow of the evening. Guests should note that some areas are connected by a central spiral staircase and narrow internal corridors, and event logistics require coordination between levels.

### Facilities

* Capacity: 40–150 guests
* 55 guest rooms (multiple configurations)
* Elevator access to principal levels
* Grand gallery and breakout spaces
* Panoramic city and mountain views
* Elevated terraces for receptions
* Eastern balcony suited to acoustic performances
* Multi-level catering and event setup
* Historic architecture and distinctive interiors
* Year-round operation

# Party Tree Hall and Grounds

Source: https://federicab03.github.io/ARGO_Usecase/venues/partyfield.html

Intimate Alpine Venue

A historic community gathering space in the heart of Michel Delving, combining rustic charm, open-air surroundings, and practical event facilities for relaxed, convivial celebrations.

**Party Tree Hall and Grounds**

Where fellowship blooms beneath open skies. Warm, welcoming, and effortlessly convivial, the Party Tree Hall brings together shared tables, open meadows, and the spirit of the Shire.

Nestled among the green of central Michel Delving, the Party Tree Hall offers a welcoming setting for an evening of food, music, and fellowship. Its spacious ground-level reception area provides direct loading access for catering and music equipment, while the adjoining courtyard offers an open-air extension of the gathering with clear sightlines and easy guest circulation. Dedicated preparation and staging areas remain separate from attendee spaces, supporting smooth transitions throughout the evening.

### Facilities

* Capacity: 20–90 guests
* 32 en-suite bedrooms
* Fully accessible ground-level layout
* Direct loading access for event setup
* Indoor hall and adjoining courtyard
* Dedicated food preparation and staging areas
* Clear emergency access routes
* Hiking trails from the property
* Available year-round
